Our new project rolls out Wednesday. Indie Circuit is a free map of every independent movie theater in the United States that books films outside the studio system: 1,592 theaters in all 50 states, each listing built from what the theater publishes on its own site.

For filmmakers with a finished feature, it is the pitch list. For theaters, it is a free page that tells filmmakers what you are looking for. On January 11 the Screening Room opens, where filmmakers post a feature and vetted theaters request it.
