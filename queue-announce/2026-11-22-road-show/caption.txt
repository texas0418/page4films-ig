The road show is back. Indie filmmakers who can't land a distributor are booking theaters themselves and taking their films on tour, town by town.

IndieWire covered it last year: Hundreds of Beavers took a road show through several states and earned more than five times its $150,000 budget, and filmmakers behind Cheat, A Hard Place and The Occult did the same with their own tours.

Every one of those tours starts with the same question: which theaters will book you?

Our project Indie Circuit mapped 1,902 independent theaters across the US, with who books each one and how they want to be pitched. Free, no sign-up: circuit.page4films.com
