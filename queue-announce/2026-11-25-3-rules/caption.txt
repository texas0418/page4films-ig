Finishing your first feature? Here are 3 rules from @filmthreat about what happens after the film is done:

1. Keep your rights. A cheap sale can cost you control of the film and everything it earns later.
2. Distribution has changed. You can reach an audience without giving up ownership, online and in theaters that book independent films.
3. Think catalog, not blockbuster. A body of work you own can be worth more over time than one big swing.
