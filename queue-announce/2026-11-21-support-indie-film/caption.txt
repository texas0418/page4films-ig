Independent films tell the stories studios pass on, and independent theaters are where they get seen. Both only last if people show up.

Six things you can do:
1. See a film at your local indie theater this month.
2. Bring a friend who has never been.
3. Rent or buy indie films from the filmmakers.
4. Share the films you love. It is their marketing.
5. Join or donate to your local nonprofit cinema.
6. Get your local indie theater to join Indie Circuit.

Not sure where your nearest independent theater is? Our project Indie Circuit mapped 1,902 of them across the US, free: circuit.page4films.com

What's the indie theater you love? Tell us in the comments.
